Privacy, in plain words

Last updated: November 26, 2026. Glow Up is for teens 13 and up. Tell a parent or guardian you are using it.

Without an account

Everything stays in your browser on your device. We do not see it.

If you sign in with Google

  • What we save: your Glow Up progress (quiz answers, workouts done, streaks, badges, check-ins) plus your Google name and email so you can sign in.
  • What we never ask for or save: your weight, height, body photos or camera video.
  • Where: Google Firebase, a cloud service run by Google.
  • Who can see it: only you, when signed in. We never sell it or show ads with it.

The camera coach

The camera video stays on your device. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent.

Delete your data

In the app, go to Progress, then Your account, then Delete my account. That removes your saved progress from our server right away. You can also email yvonnechen16412@gmail.com.

Not medical advice

Glow Up gives general fitness and food tips. It is not medical advice.
