Questions

FAQ

Is Glow Up free?

Yes. It is free while we test it. Later a membership will be RM10 a month. Check in 30 days in a row and you earn a free month.

What is the Glow Score?

A score out of 100 for your day. You get points for moving, water, sleep, a smart food pick, and keeping sugar drinks low. 70 or more is a great day. It never counts calories.

Do I need a gym?

No. Every plan works in a gym, a park or your bedroom. You pick.

Is this a weight loss app?

It is a glow-up app. We track strength, energy, streaks and confidence, not calories.

Do I need protein powder?

No. Real food is enough. Our Menu Hacks show easy protein foods like eggs, chicken, tofu and yogurt.

What if I miss a day?

That is okay. You start with a Streak Saver, and you earn another every 7 days in a row (you can hold 2). If you miss a day, a Saver protects your streak automatically. Low energy? Lazy Mode is 2 easy minutes and still counts.

Do sports and PE count?

Yes. Log sports practice, PE, walking, biking or dancing on the Workouts page. It all counts toward your streak and your weekly goal.

Is this medical advice?

No. Glow Up gives general tips. Talk to a parent and a doctor before big changes, and stop if anything hurts.
